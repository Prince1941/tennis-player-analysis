Understanding Shot-by-Shot Analysis

CourtVision AI continuously processes the entire video to detect each individual tennis stroke in a rally.

Features Computed

  • Sequential Shot Detection: Kinetic wrist and arm velocity curves identify each impact peak throughout the rally.
  • Dynamic Biomechanical Joint Angles: Joint angles are calculated across video frames and independently aggregated for every detected shot.
  • Trunk Rotation Diff: Measures hip-shoulder separation in degrees for each stroke.
  • Trunk Lean: Computes forward/lateral tilt angle relative to the center of gravity in radians.
  • Stance Width: Represents lateral ankle distance relative to hip width.

Model Architecture

The neural network is a TennisTransformer classifier consisting of:

  • Input Dimension: 50 features per frame (39 3D MediaPipe world metric coordinates + 11 derived biomechanical variables).
  • Sequence Length: 30 temporal frames normalized and centered around each stroke velocity peak.
  • Heads: Multitask head predicting:
    • Direction: Center, Cross Court, Down The Line
    • Stroke Type: Backhand, Forehand
    • Posture Quality: Bad, Good

Weights file: model_random_split.pt.
